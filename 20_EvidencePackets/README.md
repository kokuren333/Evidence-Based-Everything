# 20_EvidencePackets

Durable evidence packets for published or already-published EBE articles.

Do not use this directory for draft research, exploratory source discovery, rough claim tables, or failed-gate work. Those belong under `_working/`.

On successful publish, promote the final article-level evidence packet here from `_working/evidence_packets/`. Each packet should connect the published article to its accepted sources, major claims, contradictions, limitations, and relevant audit/publish logs.
